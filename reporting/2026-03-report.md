# [Report] Agentic Commerce – March 2026

**Working Group:** WG Agentic Commerce

**Reporting period:** March 2026

**Chair(s):** Ilya Grigorik (Shopify) & Rahul Bansal (OpenAI)

---

## Summary

The Working Group converted the kickoff inventory into a lifecycle-oriented model and agreed to map existing protocols and gaps before making recommendations. The emerging frame covered discovery, checkout and negotiation, payment, and post-purchase, with identity and authorization, human oversight, and interoperability treated as cross-cutting concerns.

## Progress Against Objectives

The group established a problem-first method for its landscape work: define the user journey and required capabilities, map existing protocols against that journey, and identify overlaps, gaps, and handoffs between phases. Product discovery was selected as the first deep dive, while the broader landscape map was expected to capture roles, responsibilities, protocol interplay, and missing capabilities.

March also produced concrete inputs for the charter. Participants identified the need for a neutral ecosystem view rather than another competing protocol, discussed strategic frameworks and implementation guidance as possible outputs, and documented missing perspectives that should shape the limits and caveats of the group’s work.

## Blockers and Risks

The protocol inventory and offline pre-work remained incomplete. Representation was strongest among platforms and payment providers and lighter among merchants and marketplaces, networks and issuers, regulatory and compliance practitioners, and logistics or fulfillment operators.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

None.

## Next Month's Focus

1. Compare feed-based and live-query approaches to product discovery, including source discovery, normalization, freshness, and trust.
2. Turn the lifecycle and gap-analysis work into a draft charter with clear scope and deliverables.
3. Examine checkout and negotiation through ACP and UCP, including extensibility, transport bindings, and human handoff.
