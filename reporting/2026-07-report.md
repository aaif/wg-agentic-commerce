# [Report] Agentic Commerce – July 2026

**Working Group:** WG Agentic Commerce

**Reporting period:** July 2026

**Chair(s):** Ilya Grigorik (Shopify) & Rahul Bansal (OpenAI)

---

## Summary

The Working Group began its post-purchase capability review and explored how agent wallets and payment mandates fit into the broader commerce lifecycle. The discussion showed that order visibility and payment execution are necessary but insufficient: agents also need policy, consent, delegated-authority, negotiation, and mutation capabilities around the transaction.

## Progress Against Objectives

Post-purchase analysis advanced the **Capability Map** by separating policy discovery and order observation from eligibility checks, remedy negotiation, buyer authorization, and state-changing requests. UCP served as one concrete input for examining structured policies, presentation messages, order events, and adjustment ledgers, while exposing the gap between reading an order and initiating a cancellation, return, refund, or other remedy.

The wallet discussion added useful dimensions for the **Gap Analysis** and **Reference Architecture**. Wallets and x402-style payment responses were mapped as contributors of payment or credential capabilities rather than complete commerce systems. AP2 mandates illustrated how bounded intent might accompany payment, while merchant receipt, validation, transaction binding, and renewed approval remained open integration questions.

## Blockers and Risks

Post-purchase expertise and representation remained limited, and the wallet observations were exploratory rather than comprehensive. These gaps create completeness risk for the Capability Map as its September target approaches.
