# [Report] Agentic Commerce – February 2026

**Working Group:** WG Agentic Commerce

**Reporting period:** February 2026

**Chair(s):** Ilya Grigorik (Shopify) & Rahul Bansal (OpenAI)

---

## Summary

The Working Group launched with an initial survey of the agentic commerce landscape and the problems a neutral industry forum might address. The kickoff surfaced a broad set of concerns, including product discoverability, transaction execution, identity and delegated authority, fraud and liability, human oversight, merchant integration burden, and disputes.

## Progress Against Objectives

The kickoff produced an initial capability and actor inventory spanning consumers, agents, merchants, wallets, payment providers, banks, and payment networks. Existing efforts including UCP, ACP, AP2, and x402 were identified as inputs, alongside cross-cutting needs such as capability discovery, agent authorization, reputation, dispute resolution, traceability, micropayments, and conformance testing.

The discussion highlighted protocol fragmentation and merchant integration burden as recurring ecosystem concerns. It also underscored the need to turn the broad inventory into a bounded scope, priority problems, and clear expected outcomes for the community.

## Blockers and Risks

The group did not yet have an agreed definition of agentic commerce, a bounded scope, prioritized use cases, or owned deliverables. The breadth of the initial problem inventory created a risk of attempting to cover too much before narrowing the mandate and agreeing clear priorities.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

None.

## Next Month's Focus

1. Convert the kickoff inventory into a shared commerce lifecycle and identify cross-cutting concerns.
2. Define the Working Group’s scope, success criteria, and candidate deliverables.
3. Establish a protocol landscape and gap-analysis method and select the first phase for deeper study.
