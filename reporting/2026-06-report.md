# [Report] Agentic Commerce – June 2026

**Working Group:** WG Agentic Commerce

**Reporting period:** June 2026

**Chair(s):** Ilya Grigorik (Shopify) & Rahul Bansal (OpenAI)

---

## Summary

The Working Group examined how fraud and trust signals change when a shopper acts through an agent and transaction context is distributed across more systems. The discussion focused on preserving useful evidence across agent, platform, merchant, payment-provider, and risk-system boundaries while avoiding indiscriminate data sharing.

## Progress Against Objectives

The **Capability Map** advanced around participant-specific signal visibility, lifecycle handoffs, and the distinction between agent identity and risk, shopper identity and risk, and evidence binding the two to a delegation and transaction. Intent evidence, signal provenance, and baseline versus risk-triggered exchange were identified as additional dimensions for protocol comparison.

The session sharpened the **Gap Analysis** by separating signal interoperability from fraud-model internals. Candidate approaches included a portable baseline of bounded facts or attestations, additional evidence or human confirmation for elevated-risk cases, and platform-local checks that share limited outcomes downstream. The analysis also reinforced dependencies on the Identity and Trust, Security and Privacy, and Observability and Traceability Working Groups.

## Blockers and Risks

Deliverable ownership remained unspecified. The planned post-purchase discussion was deferred, leaving a material capability area underexplored and creating completeness risk for the Capability Map.
