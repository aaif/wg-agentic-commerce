# [Report] Agentic Commerce – May 2026

**Working Group:** WG Agentic Commerce

**Reporting period:** May 2026

**Chair(s):** Ilya Grigorik (Shopify) & Rahul Bansal (OpenAI)

---

## Summary

The Working Group finalized its charter and shifted from scope definition into deliverable-oriented protocol analysis. May’s technical deep dive compared payment-handler models in ACP and UCP with the narrower Machine Payments Protocol (MPP), clarifying shared abstractions and the gaps that must be resolved before cross-protocol composition can be recommended.

## Progress Against Objectives

The approved charter established the **Agentic Commerce Capability Map** as the first planned deliverable and confirmed the group’s protocol-neutral focus on interfaces and signals across discovery, delegated authority, checkout, payment, fraud signaling, and post-purchase.

Payment analysis advanced the capability model around three reusable concepts: **handler**, **instrument**, and **credential**. The discussion compared static payment-capability discovery with transaction-specific eligibility, mapped delegated tokenization and sensitive-data boundaries, and examined how custom methods and wallets attach to checkout. MPP’s challenge–credential–receipt flow was considered as a possible payment-handler composition pattern, while lifecycle differences, trust assumptions, adapters, and validation remained inputs to the Gap Analysis rather than evidence of interoperability.

## Blockers and Risks

No technical blocker was identified. Deliverable ownership remained unspecified, creating schedule risk against the charter’s target dates for the Capability Map and recommendations to the TC.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

None.

## Next Month's Focus

1. Examine how fraud and trust signals survive across shopper, agent platform, merchant, payment provider, and risk-system boundaries.
2. Separate agent identity and risk from shopper identity and risk, including evidence that binds the agent, delegation, intent, and transaction.
3. Explore a privacy-aware baseline of portable signals and risk-triggered requests for additional context.
